# Seclusa Blocks

A falling-blocks puzzle. Shapes drop from the top, you slide them left and
right, and you clear rows by filling them up.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no adverts, nothing watching how
you play.

## Features

- Choose how hard you want it to start, from 1 to 100
- Left-handed or right-handed play
- Clears a row when you fill it, with extra points for clearing four at once
- Extra bonus every ten rows
- Play with your thumbs or a keyboard
- Music and sound effects, both off until you turn them on
- Add it to your home screen and it works with no internet connection
- Picks up where you left off if the page reloads by accident

## Usage

Open it and press start. Move with the arrow keys, or drag the on-screen pad on
a phone. Pressing down drops a shape straight to the bottom.
